Business Problem
Frequent flight delays reduce customer satisfaction, disrupt schedules, and increase operational costs. The dashboard helps answer three key questions:
- Which airports generate the highest delays?
- What are the main causes of delays?
- Which aircraft and routes require operational attention?
Dashboards Included
1. Top Delayed Airports
Compares average departure and arrival delays across major airports to identify operational bottlenecks.
Business value: highlights airports where staffing, boarding, or turnaround processes should be optimized.
2. Delay Reasons Analysis
Breaks down delays by operational cause (air traffic control, maintenance, weather).
Business value: distinguishes controllable operational issues from external factors.
3. Schedule Recovery Analysis
Compares departure and arrival delays by aircraft type to evaluate whether delays are reduced during flight operations.
Business value: measures operational recovery efficiency.
4. Route Performance
Analyzes average arrival delays across origin–destination pairs.
Business value: identifies routes that may require schedule adjustments or additional buffer time.
Key Insights
- Certain airports consistently show higher departure delays.
- Maintenance and air traffic control are the most significant delay drivers.
- Delay patterns differ by aircraft type and route.
- Some routes experience systematically higher arrival delays than others.
Recommended Actions
- Increase operational resources at high-delay airports.
- Improve maintenance planning and aircraft turnaround processes.
- Review schedules for consistently delayed routes.
- Monitor aircraft with persistent delay patterns.
Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers
- Conditional Formatting
Outcome
The final dashboard provides an interactive operational monitoring tool that supports data-driven decisions aimed at reducing delays and improving airline punctuality.
